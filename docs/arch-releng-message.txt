Subject: Per-machine installer defaults, and whether a graphical medium is in scope

Hello,

I maintain an unofficial graphical installer for Arch Linux, built on archiso:
it uses the releng profile with mkarchiso, boots through syslinux on BIOS and
systemd-boot on UEFI, and installs the system with pacstrap and arch-chroot
following the installation guide. It is called Kiln Setup and it is explicitly
not an official Arch medium; I am not asking you to adopt it.

I am writing because building it raised two questions that are really about
archiso and the official medium, and I would rather ask than assume.

1. Defaults that follow the machine.

Most of what an installer has to ask is determined by the hardware it is running
on: the desktop that fits the RAM, the microcode package, the boot loader from
the firmware mode, the swap size, the graphics driver, and which firmware
packages are needed. Arch's linux-firmware is now a metapackage over the vendor
splits, so a machine with only an NVIDIA card still pulls roughly 28 MiB of
amdgpu firmware, and an install can instead take linux-firmware-<vendor> for the
PCI vendors actually present. Instance detection here is just lspci -nn plus
/proc and /sys.

Is per-machine narrowing of the firmware set something archinstall would want?
It is a small, contained change (map PCI vendor id to the split package, fall
back to the metapackage when nothing is detected), and it is the kind of thing
that helps the small-disk and thin-client cases.

2. Scope of the official medium.

I understand the live medium is deliberately minimal and that archinstall is the
official guided installer. Is a graphical front end on the official medium
something releng would consider at all, or is the console medium a fixed
decision? If it is fixed, I would find it useful to know whether that is a
policy choice (which I will respect and stop asking about) or a matter of
maintenance capacity, and whether anything short of a full graphical medium -
for example a documented, supported way for derivative media to reuse the
releng profile - would be welcome.

Either way I am not proposing to change anything on archiso's side today; the
project builds its own image with the stock releng profile and ships no patched
Arch packages.

The project keeps Arch's branding out of its own name and states in its window,
its boot menu and its release notes that it is unofficial and unaffiliated.

Repository: https://github.com/shelbycountyfc-rgb/arch-gui-installer

Thanks for the work on archiso and the release images - building a medium that
boots on both firmware modes and still reaches a shell when something fails is
more delicate than it looks.
